# Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy

**Document Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** March 2024  
**Review Date:** March 2025  
**Policy Owner:** Maya Thompson, Chief Executive Officer  
**Last Updated:** February 28, 2024  

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes Horizon Foundation's commitment to safeguarding and protecting children, young people, and vulnerable adults who participate in our programs or come into contact with our staff, volunteers, and partners. This policy applies to all Horizon Foundation personnel, including employees, volunteers, contractors, board members, and partner organisations delivering services on our behalf.

The policy covers all Horizon Foundation activities including youth employment programs, digital literacy sessions, community health initiatives, emergency relief services, and any outreach or engagement activities conducted across metropolitan and regional Western Australia.

## 2. Legislative and Regulatory Framework

This policy is developed in accordance with:

- **Working with Children (Criminal Record Checking) Act 2004 (WA)**
- **Children and Community Services Act 2004 (WA)**
- **Criminal Code Act Compilation Act 1913 (WA)**
- **Reportable Conduct Scheme** (proposed implementation 2025)
- **Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC) Governance Standards**

All staff and volunteers must comply with mandatory reporting obligations under Western Australian legislation.

## 3. Definitions

**Child:** Any person under the age of 18 years.

**Vulnerable Adult:** Any person aged 18 years or over who may be at risk of abuse or neglect due to disability, mental health conditions, social isolation, or other circumstances that may impact their ability to protect themselves.

**Child Abuse:** Any act committed against a child involving physical abuse, sexual abuse, emotional abuse, neglect, or exposure to family violence.

**Safeguarding:** Proactive measures to protect children and vulnerable adults from harm, including creating safe environments and building protective cultures.

**Regulated Activity:** Any role involving direct contact with children or vulnerable adults as defined under the Working with Children Act.

## 4. Our Commitment

Horizon Foundation is committed to:

- Providing safe environments for all program participants
- Implementing robust screening and training processes
- Maintaining a culture where concerns can be raised safely
- Responding appropriately and promptly to safeguarding concerns
- Supporting survivors of abuse and their families
- Continuously improving our safeguarding practices

## 5. Working with Children Requirements

### 5.1 Working with Children Check (WWCC) Requirements

All personnel in regulated activities must hold a current Working with Children Check before commencing duties. This includes:

- **Direct program delivery staff** (Jess Warburton, Programs Director, and program coordinators)
- **Volunteers** engaging with youth employment participants
- **Digital literacy trainers** working with seniors (as some sessions include intergenerational components)
- **Community health workers** providing family-based services
- **Emergency relief volunteers** conducting home visits

### 5.2 WWCC Administration Process

Pat Moriarty (Volunteer & Community Coordinator) maintains the WWCC register and renewal calendar. All checks must be verified before commencement and monitored for expiry dates. Current renewal backlog from Department of Justice delays requires advance planning of 8-10 weeks for new applications.

**Exception:** Board members participating only in governance activities are exempt unless directly involved in program delivery.

### 5.3 Interstate and Visitor Requirements

Personnel from other states must provide equivalent clearances. Temporary visitors (researchers, partner organisation staff) require supervision when in contact with children.

## 6. Recruitment and Screening

### 6.1 Position Risk Assessment

All roles are assessed for safeguarding risk levels:

- **High Risk:** Direct unsupervised contact with children/vulnerable adults
- **Medium Risk:** Supervised contact or access to premises where programs operate
- **Low Risk:** No direct contact with vulnerable populations

### 6.2 Recruitment Process

For medium and high-risk positions:

1. **Position descriptions** explicitly state safeguarding requirements
2. **Interview questions** address commitment to child safety
3. **Reference checks** specifically enquire about suitability for work with vulnerable populations
4. **Probationary period** includes safeguarding competency assessment

Craig Donovan (Finance & Operations Manager) coordinates recruitment screening with input from relevant program managers.

### 6.3 Volunteer Screening

All volunteers complete:
- Application form with referee details
- Informal interview with Pat Moriarty
- WWCC where required
- Safeguarding training (online module plus face-to-face session)
- Supervised integration period

## 7. Training and Education

### 7.1 Mandatory Training Requirements

**All Staff and Volunteers:**
- Safeguarding awareness training (annually)
- Recognising signs of abuse and neglect
- Appropriate boundaries and professional relationships
- Incident reporting procedures

**Program Delivery Staff:**
- Trauma-informed practice approaches
- De-escalation techniques
- Cultural competency (particularly relevant for our Aboriginal and CALD community engagement)

### 7.2 Training Schedule

Training is delivered quarterly with additional sessions for new starters. Jess Warburton coordinates program-specific training while Pat Moriarty manages volunteer orientation.

*Note: 2024 training calendar requires updating following the departure of our external training provider in December 2023. Interim arrangements using Department of Communities resources are in place.*

## 8. Creating Safe Environments

### 8.1 Physical Environment Standards

**Program Venues:**
- Open-door policies where possible
- Adequate lighting and visibility
- Appropriate privacy measures for counselling/support services
- Secure storage for participant information
- Clear sight lines in youth employment training spaces

**Home Visiting Protocols:**
- Two-person teams for initial visits
- Clear identification and purpose explanation
- Respect for cultural and religious practices
- Emergency contact procedures

### 8.2 Digital Safety

Given our digital literacy programs and online engagement:

- Age-appropriate content filtering
- Supervised internet access during training
- Clear social media interaction guidelines
- Secure handling of participant photos/videos
- GDPR-compliant data management (even though not legally required, best practice for donor confidence)

## 9. Professional Boundaries

### 9.1 Appropriate Relationships

Staff and volunteers must maintain professional boundaries including:

- No personal relationships with current participants under 18
- No private contact outside program activities
- No gift-giving beyond approved recognition items
- Clear physical contact guidelines (handshakes appropriate, hugs only when initiated by participant)

### 9.2 Power Dynamics

Recognition of inherent power imbalances, particularly in:
- Emergency relief provision
- Employment mentoring relationships
- Technology training with older adults
- Health information provision

## 10. Incident Reporting and Response

### 10.1 Mandatory Reporting Obligations

All staff must report suspected child abuse to:
- **Department of Communities** (Child Protection and Family Support)
- **WA Police** (if immediate danger)
- **Maya Thompson** (internal reporting)

Reporting obligations apply regardless of where the suspected abuse occurs (within or outside Horizon programs).

### 10.2 Internal Incident Management

**Immediate Response (within 24 hours):**
1. Ensure immediate safety of affected person
2. Contact emergency services if required
3. Document initial concerns
4. Notify Maya Thompson or designated deputy

**Follow-up Response (within 72 hours):**
1. Complete detailed incident report
2. Liaise with relevant authorities
3. Implement support measures for affected participants
4. Review circumstances and preventive measures

### 10.3 Documentation Requirements

Incident reports must include:
- Date, time, and location
- Persons involved and witnesses
- Objective description of events
- Actions taken
- Recommendations for follow-up

All documentation stored securely with restricted access (Craig Donovan maintains incident register).

## 11. Supporting Survivors

Horizon Foundation commits to:

- Believing and supporting anyone who discloses abuse
- Connecting survivors with appropriate specialist services
- Maintaining confidentiality while fulfilling legal obligations
- Adjusting program delivery to meet ongoing participation needs
- Providing staff debriefing and support following incidents

**Current Service Partnerships:**
- Communicare (counselling services)
- Relationships Australia WA (family support)
- Aboriginal Family Law Service (culturally appropriate support)
- Youth Legal Service (advocacy support)

*Note: Partnerships under review as part of broader service mapping exercise led by Amara Osei.*

## 12. Managing Allegations Against Staff/Volunteers

### 12.1 Internal Allegations Process

When allegations involve Horizon personnel:

1. **Immediate suspension** from duties involving vulnerable populations
2. **Independent investigation** (external investigator if involving senior staff)
3. **Cooperation** with police and regulatory investigations
4. **Clear communication** with affected participants and families
5. **Support** for all parties during investigation

### 12.2 Natural Justice Principles

Investigations will:
- Provide fair opportunity for response
- Maintain confidentiality where possible
- Avoid predetermined outcomes
- Respect legal professional privilege
- Document decisions and rationale

## 13. Information Sharing and Confidentiality

### 13.1 Confidentiality Principles

Information is shared only:
- With explicit consent (where legally permissible)
- To fulfill mandatory reporting obligations
- To ensure immediate safety
- Between relevant team members for service coordination

### 13.2 Record Keeping

Safeguarding records maintained separately from general program files with:
- Restricted access permissions
- 7-year retention period (longer if legal proceedings)
- Secure destruction protocols
- Regular backup and security reviews

## 14. Cultural Responsiveness

Horizon Foundation acknowledges the over-representation of Aboriginal children in child protection systems and commits to:

- Culturally responsive program design
- Partnership with Aboriginal community organisations
- Recognition of family and kinship structures
- Trauma-informed approaches acknowledging historical impacts
- Connection to appropriate cultural support services

Similar considerations apply to our work with culturally and linguistically diverse communities, recognizing different help-seeking patterns and family structures.
